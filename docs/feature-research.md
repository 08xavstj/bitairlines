# What other airline and airport games do, and what Bit Airlines should take

Research done in October 2026 from store pages, manuals and reviews. Four kinds of game were looked at: airline tycoons, airport builders, flight-sim career add-ons (where bush flying lives) and small network puzzle games. The recommendations are ranked for this game: a bush start, pixel art, one player, an iPhone.

## The games

### Airline tycoons

| Game | What it does well |
|---|---|
| [Pocket Planes](https://www.imore.com/pocket-planes-iphone-ipad-review) | Jobs to pick up at each city (passengers and cargo), paying for a spot at each airport and upgrading it for more capacity, plane parts (fuel, engine, frame), advertising when a city runs out of jobs, worldwide group events. Reviews say it peaks early and loses depth later. |
| [AirlineSim](https://manual.airwaysim.com/simulation-documentation/alliances) | Real airport slots and capacity, traffic rights, cabin layouts and service profiles, price tuning, alliances and interline deals. |
| [Airline Manager 4](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1641650/Airline_Manager/) | 360 aircraft and 3,600 airports, an Easy mode and a Realism mode where runway length matters, buying fuel and CO2 quotas when cheap, scheduled repairs and checks, hubs. |
| [Aerobiz Supersonic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aerobiz) | Before a route opens you need a slot at both ends and a plane with the range; slots are won by sending representatives to negotiate, and can be given back. Historical scenarios. |
| [Airline Tycoon 2](https://store.steampowered.com/app/201490) | Random events to profit from (a special VIP flight, volcanic ash, unrest), a plane editor for cheap or luxury aircraft, avatars with different strengths, rivals who sabotage you. |
| [AirTycoon](https://www.appspy.com/review/3916/airtycoon-airline-management) | Each route has schedule, fare and service level; 3 to 7 computer rivals; several play modes and difficulties. |

### Airport builders

| Game | What it does well |
|---|---|
| [Airport CEO](https://store.steampowered.com/app/673610/Airport_CEO/) | Build runways, gates and terminals, sign deals with airlines, keep passengers happy because happy passengers spend; stressed staff, breakdowns and bad weather cause delays and emergency landings. |
| [SimAirport](https://www.xbox.com/en-us/games/store/simairport/9pn5pq4d6glt) | Airline contracts, a daily flight schedule, fuel and baggage systems, research to unlock tools. A Career mode and a Sandbox mode with no money worries. |
| [Sky Haven](https://www.gog.com/en/game/sky_haven_tycoon_airport_simulator) | Play from 1916 to today, researching new technology; pick your strategy (remote or bridged stands, charter or scheduled, big or small aircraft); choose your own difficulty. |
| [World of Airports](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/world-of-airports/id1462642534) | The turnaround on the apron: stands, fuelling, handling, boarding; send flights to other players' airports; leaderboards. Proof that watching the apron works on a phone. |

### Flight-sim careers (the closest thing to running a bush operation)

| Game | What it does well |
|---|---|
| [FSEconomy](https://www.helisimmer.com/tutorials/fs-economy-as-fun-as-it-gets-part-1/) | A job board at every airport; renting before owning; not every airport sells fuel; player-owned bases (FBOs) that need building materials and supplies and earn ground fees. |
| [OnAir Company](https://www.simflight.com/?p=121231) | Run an airline or a base that sells fuel, maintenance and aircraft; hire pilots who fly for you; reputation sets your loan interest rate. |
| [Air Hauler 2](https://justflight.com/product/air-hauler-2-microsoft-flight-simulator) | A cargo job board, bases, buying or leasing, hiring computer pilots, even trading goods and factories at your bases. |
| [Flight Simulator 2024 career](https://msfsaddons.com/2024/09/19/heres-how-the-career-mode-works-in-microsoft-flight-simulator-2024/) | Kinds of work: air ambulance, search and rescue, firefighting, cargo, VIP charter, skydiving, aerial photography. Jobs need a rating, an aircraft class or a reputation. |

### Small network and traffic games

| Game | What it does well |
|---|---|
| [Fly Corp](https://ladiesgamers.com/fly-corp-review/) | Real cities with real populations; every passenger has a destination and changes planes if there is no direct flight; planes and airports have a capacity, and an overloaded one ends the run. |
| [Mini Airways](https://www.pcgamesn.com/mini-airways/out-now-steam) | At the end of each day you pick one of three upgrades; special visitors (a VIP flight) force a reshuffle; maps unlock one by one; Classic, Hardcore and Sandbox modes. |

## What Bit Airlines already has

- A remote bush start with real communities, gravel strips, floatplanes and freight as half the business.
- Aircraft that fly their routes on their own, with the game stopping only for real problems.
- A customisable airline identity carried onto every aircraft.
- Demand fitted to real traffic, a forecast before every route, a guided first route, chiptune sound.

## Recommendations, in the order I would build them

| # | Feature | Seen in | Why it fits here | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Job board.** One-off jobs at airports alongside your routes: medevac to the hospital town, a mail run, fuel drums for a village, a crew change at a mine, a fishing lodge charter on floats, a survey flight. Each job has a deadline, a pay rate, and needs the right aircraft (floats, gravel, range). | Pocket Planes, FSEconomy, Air Hauler 2, Flight Simulator career | It is what real bush operators live on. It uses the systems already built (aircraft limits, the map, the clock) and gives a reason to act between route decisions. | Medium |
| 2 | **Bases at remote airports.** Build at a strip you serve: a fuel depot (removes the remote fuel premium there), a hangar (repairs and checks without flying home), runway lights (night flights), a gravel extension (bigger aircraft). Bases cost upkeep. | FSEconomy bases, OnAir, Pocket Planes airport upgrades | Turns the setting into decisions, and gives money a use besides more aircraft. | Medium |
| 3 | **Pick one of three perks at each new certificate level.** For example cheaper fuel at home, quicker turnarounds, more reputation in one region, a free permit. | Mini Airways | Nearly free to build, makes every level-up a choice, and makes two airlines play differently. | Low |
| 4 | **Easy, Realism and Sandbox modes.** Easy ignores runway, surface and weather limits; Realism adds fuel only where it is really sold; Sandbox has no money limit for people who want to build and paint. | Airline Manager 4, SimAirport, Mini Airways | Widens the audience without watering down the main game. | Low |
| 5 | **Events with a choice.** A forest fire evacuation, an early thaw that closes the ice road (freight surge), volcanic ash, a film crew charter, the Arctic Winter Games. Say yes, say no, or bid. | Airline Tycoon 2, Mini Airways, Airport CEO | Gives the north a voice and makes the pause-on-issue system pay off with good news as well as bad. | Medium |
| 6 | **Connecting passengers through hubs.** People ride two legs if there is no direct flight, and a hub earns from it. | Fly Corp, AirlineSim, Airline Manager 4 | Rewards building a network, not just single routes. Fly Corp shows it can stay readable. | High |
| 7 | **Slots and capacity at big airports.** Small strips are free; a busy airport has a limited number of slots that cost money or have to be won. | Aerobiz Supersonic, Fly Corp, Pocket Planes | A natural brake in the late game and a reason to grow from the edges in. | Medium |
| 8 | **Eras.** Start in 1955 with a DC-3 and watch the Twin Otter, the 737 and the 747 arrive in their real years, while old types age out. | Sky Haven, Aerobiz scenarios | The catalogue already has the history; it turns collecting aircraft into the spine of a long game. | Medium |
| 9 | **Skis, floats and kits.** Swap floats for skis in winter, add a gravel kit or a STOL kit, convert to freight. | Pocket Planes parts, Airline Tycoon 2 plane editor | Very northern and very visual: the same aircraft looks different by season. | Medium |
| 10 | **Scenarios with goals.** "Supply Nunavut before freeze-up", "Serve every village on the Yukon-Kuskokwim delta", "Run a profitable jet route by 1975". Medals for time and money. | Aerobiz, Mini Airways maps, Airlines Manager challenges | Replay value and short sessions that suit a phone. | Medium |
| 11 | **Pilots as people.** Hire pilots with type ratings and hours; they have duty limits and can be trained up. | OnAir, Air Hauler 2, AirlineSim | Deepens the issues loop (sickness, no rated pilot) and makes growth cost more than money. | High |
| 12 | **Service and on-time record.** Snacks or not, seat comfort, on-time percentage, all feeding reputation. | AirTycoon, Airport CEO | Gives the fare slider a partner and reputation a cause. | Low |
| 13 | **Home base scene.** A small animated apron at your home airport: aircraft taxiing, fuelling and boarding, the departures board. | World of Airports, Airport CEO | The pixel-art payoff; pleasant to leave open. | Medium |
| 14 | **Named computer airlines** on the busy routes, with fares, fleets and a rankings table. No sabotage. | AirTycoon, AirlineSim, Airline Tycoon 2 | Makes the late game a contest. | High |
| 15 | **Fuel market.** Buy ahead when it is cheap, a price chart, an oil shock event. | Airline Manager 4, Airline Club | A small, readable strategy layer on an existing number. | Low |

## What not to copy

- **Terminal building down to the bins and sinks** (Airport CEO, SimAirport). Wrong scale for an airline game on a phone; a base you upgrade in a few steps gets the feeling.
- **Real-time air traffic control as the main loop** (Mini Airways, World of Airports). It fights "aircraft fly on their own".
- **Premium currency that skips waiting** (Pocket Planes Bux).
- **Sabotaging rivals** (Airline Tycoon). Funny once, out of tone for this game.
- **Online-only play** (AirlineSim, Airline Manager 4). Leaderboards and shared liveries get most of the social value.

## My suggested next three

1. **Job board** (1): the heart of bush flying, and it reuses what exists.
2. **Perks at each certificate level** (3) together with **Easy, Realism and Sandbox modes** (4): both are small and change how the game feels straight away.
3. **Bases at remote airports** (2): gives money a purpose in the middle game and pairs with the job board (a fuel depot makes jobs to that village pay).
