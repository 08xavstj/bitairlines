# What other airline games do, and what Bit Airlines should take

Research done in October 2026 from store pages and reviews, plus the game's own design notes. The recommendations are ranked for this game: a bush start, pixel art, one player, an iPhone.

## The games

| Game | What it is | What it does well |
|---|---|---|
| [Pocket Planes](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/pocket-planes-airline-tycoon/id491994942) | Casual mobile airline, 250+ fictional-plane cities | Jobs to pick up and deliver (passengers and cargo), special cargo hotspots that change every couple of days, upgrading planes with parts (range, speed, weight), building and upgrading airports, advertising to lift demand, events and trading with friends ([review](https://www.imore.com/pocket-planes-iphone-ipad-review)). |
| [AirlineSim](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2715810/AirlineSim/) | Hardcore real-time online airline | Flight scheduling with real airport capacity and slots, traffic rights, 440 aircraft types, 4,500 destinations, cabin layouts and service profiles, load factors and price tuning, watching rivals, alliances and interline deals ([manual](https://manual.airwaysim.com/simulation-documentation/alliances)). |
| [Airline Manager 4](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1641650/Airline_Manager/) | Mobile multiplayer airline | Hubs, 360 aircraft, 3,600 airports, live flight tracking, scheduled repairs and maintenance, seat configuration, buying fuel and CO2 quotas when cheap, staff happiness, marketing campaigns, alliances, an Easy mode and a Realism mode where runway length matters. |
| [Airlines Manager: Plane Tycoon](https://toucharcade.com/games/airlines-manager-plane-tycoon) | Mobile tycoon with rankings | 2,700 airports, 200 aircraft models, monthly challenges with rewards, regular new aircraft, leagues. |
| [AirTycoon Online 2](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id969924164) | Turn-based airline | Historical eras, about 150 real aircraft released as time goes by, a fast Tycoon mode and a real-time Pro mode. |

## What Bit Airlines already has that they mostly do not

- A **remote bush start** with real communities, gravel strips, floatplanes and freight as half the business.
- **Automation with pauses**: aircraft fly their routes on their own and the game stops only for real problems.
- A **customisable airline identity** (pixel logo, colours, paint scheme on every aircraft, special liveries).
- A **demand model fitted to real traffic**, so thin remote routes and busy trunk routes behave like the real thing.

## Recommendations, in the order I would build them

| # | Feature | Seen in | Why it fits here | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Contracts and jobs**: medevac, mail runs, charters, seasonal tourist flights, a subsidised "essential service" a village needs. A job board with deadlines, bonuses and reputation. | Pocket Planes, Airlines Manager challenges | It is the heart of real bush flying and gives a reason to log in that is not just "add a route". | 2 | **Airport investment in remote places**: runway lighting (night flights), gravel to paved, a fuel depot (cuts the remote fuel premium), a hangar base, terminal size. | Pocket Planes (build and upgrade airports) | Turns the remote theme into decisions: spend to make a strip better and the town grows with your service. | Medium |
| 3 | **Hubs and connecting passengers**: a hub gets cheaper fees and a transfer bonus, and demand between two spokes flows through the hub. | Airline Manager 4, AirlineSim | Rewards building a network instead of single routes. Today passengers only ride one leg. | High |

| 5 | **Easy and Realism modes**: Easy ignores runway and surface limits and weather; Realism keeps them. | Airline Manager 4 | Cheap to build, widens the audience, protects the sim for people who want it. | Low |

## What not to copy

- **Premium currency that skips waiting** (Pocket Planes' Bux for instant arrival). It fights the idea of aircraft that fly on their own.
- **Pure real-time at 1 to 1** (AirlineSim). Fine for a desktop hobby, wrong for a phone; the speed controls and pauses already solve it.
- **Social trading and live multiplayer** for now. A rankings table against computer airlines gets most of the feeling.

## My suggested next three

Contracts and jobs (1), airport investment (2) and Easy and Realism modes (5): together they give the bush start more to do, use the remote theme, and the third is nearly free.
